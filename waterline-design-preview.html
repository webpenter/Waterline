What this preview is, and is not

  • Is: the layout, hierarchy, type scale, spacing and colour of the real build. The developer can lift measurements and structure directly from it.
  • Is not: final photography. Images here are horizon gradients standing in for the licensed imagery the generator pulls automatically (§13.2). Typefaces are the free stand-ins named in §13.3 — Playfair Display for Canela, Inter for the body.
  • Every control shown is specified: boat-length and draft filters, frontage sorting, the water credentials table, the berth panel and its badge, the computed empty state, the URL-encoded filter set.
  • Refinement happens after the build reaches a working state — this preview exists so nothing needs to be imagined in the meantime.
